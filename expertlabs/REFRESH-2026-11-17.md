## How the new indexes were produced

1. `git fetch origin staging`; checked out `7c181a0` detached.
2. Booted the gateway with the in-memory driver and recorded every route through Fastify's `onRoute` hook (404 routes after dropping automatic `HEAD` and `OPTIONS`).
3. Read each route file's `preHandler` for the rate-limit class, permission and Zod schemas; 29 routes registered in loops were filled in by hand from the loop code.
4. Listed every `v2_*` collection from the Firestore repository files, mapped each to its domain model, and extracted the model fields and the composite indexes from `firestore.indexes.json`.

Known limits: "session" in the Access column means a signed-in session is resolved globally and the fine-grained check is in the service layer, which was not traced route by route. Auth-adapter and helper collections (sessions, accounts, idempotency, sequence and claim documents) have no domain model and are described briefly.

# Update 2026-11-18

Everything above still stands. Since that note, the following shipped to `staging` (backend PR #198 and frontend PR #128, both merged and deployed) and is reflected in the refreshed route and collection indexes and the vendor handover:

| Area | What changed |
|---|---|
| Roles | Role ladder: `owner` > `admin` (Ops lead) > `manager` (Venue manager) > `door_supervisor` > `gate_staff` (scans and sells) > `member` (legacy read-only). Authority only flows downward: venue managers and above can invite, change, remove, revoke and resend only for roles strictly below their own; nobody changes their own role; door roles manage no one. |
| Door permissions | New narrow permission `door.floor` (today's events, guest list, door sales, registering the handset). `venue.read` (attendance report, cover-wallet records) is supervisor and above. `POST /door/devices` needs `door.floor`; listing and re-authorizing devices stay `door.manage`. |
| Temporary password | 8 characters, valid 2 days. The expiry is stored with the rotation flag and enforced at session creation and on every request; a lapsed credential cannot even rotate. Login answers a recognisable 403 (`details.temporaryPasswordExpired`). |
| Resend | New `POST /invitations/:id/resend` (SENSITIVE_COMMAND, `staff.manage`): reissues a fresh temporary password only for accounts that never chose their own, then re-emails. Route total is now **405** (404 + this one). |
| Invite email | Shows the real role label, the 2-day validity and, for door roles, how a shift starts (sign in to the scanner app, enter the door code). The accept link needs `PARTNER_DASHBOARD_URL` set on the gateway. |
| Scanner app | New "Set your password" step on first sign-in; web build deployed on Vercel (static single-page export) with the other apps' security headers and immutable asset caching; its origin must be in the gateway's `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` and `BETTER_AUTH_TRUSTED_ORIGINS`. |
| Partner dashboard | Staff invite and edit-role dialogs offer only roles below the signed-in person; pending invites have a Resend button. |

## Defect-register rows these resolve or change
- Scanner: "device registration needs `door.manage` while the app asks every staff member" is resolved (`door.floor`).
- Scanner: the app had no way to complete the forced first-login password change (the gateway answered 403 `passwordChangeRequired` to everything else) is resolved by the new screen.
- Invite emails carried no link or credentials on staging because `PARTNER_DASHBOARD_URL` was unset; it is now declared in `render.yaml`.
- Operational risks that **remain**: rate limiting is in memory in the example blueprint (`RATE_LIMIT_STORE`), the outbox gives up after about 15 s with no jitter, the webhook shares the generic nginx zone, the edge limit (10 requests/s per IP) can throttle a venue behind one IP, bank-account encryption can fall back to a development key when `ENCRYPTION_KEY` is unset, free hosting plan hibernation. Details and proposed values are in `14-vendor-handover/`.
