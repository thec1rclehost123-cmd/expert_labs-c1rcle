- **Pricing and orders:** `core/domain/pricing.test.ts` (GST on fees only, fees on the discounted subtotal, fixed promo cap, fee rates pinned), `order.test.ts` (second confirmation no-op, two different payments against one order refused, refund lock and restore, a used ticket can be voided), `core/application/checkout/payment-audit.test.ts` (unpublished event, expired/not-started/deactivated codes, redemption limits, last-seat race, `refund_requested` seat, buyer-scoped idempotency, sub-₹1), `checkout-financial-posting.test.ts`.
- **Routes:** `payment-routes.test.ts` (forged signature rejected, idempotent verify, provider order reuse, a genuine payment for one hold cannot fulfil another hold with the same total, no attempt means no confirm, currency mismatch, late-capture revive and refund, an unexpected inventory failure is not swallowed by refunding, server order id wins, attendee carry-over, concurrent confirmations), `webhook-routes.test.ts` (missing/tampered signature, single fulfilment, non-capture events acknowledged, uncorrelated capture, dual-confirmation race, ledger split once, venue share, provider fee and tax recorded, reconcile, refund and dispute facts, event-id dedup with minimised persistence, repair sweeps), `webhook-logging.test.ts` (payer data kept out of logs).
- **Finance:** `financial-posting-service.test.ts` (balanced journals, retry returns the original, changed content under one key rejected, cumulative partial refunds, chargeback hold/lose/release), `journal-transition-service.test.ts` (preview/backfill/compare/cutover/rollback, a discrepancy blocks cutover, no double payable, no recreation of paid-out liabilities), `provider-payment-service.test.ts`, `payouts-gate.test.ts` and `payouts-gate.routes.test.ts`, `razorpay-adapter.test.ts` (594 lines), `unconfigured-provider.test.ts`.
- **Firestore emulator (skipped without `FIRESTORE_EMULATOR_HOST`):** `firestore-inventory.integration.test.ts` (12/20/60-buyer races), `firestore-late-capture.integration.test.ts`, `firestore-promo-redemption.integration.test.ts`, `firestore-finance.integration.test.ts`, `firestore-repositories.integration.test.ts`. The late-payment race is only exposed reliably by widening the gap between the capacity decision and the order write, so that test injects the delay deliberately.
- `scenarios/business-flows.test.ts` (merge only). **Not on staging:** #121's `bindProviderOrder` race test, so the bind race is covered only by a fake in an open PR.
- **Not covered by any test found:** the legacy ledger never advancing; a second payment against a converted hold; `payment.failed`; the sweep-vs-conversion race; promoter-guard vs settlement divergence.
